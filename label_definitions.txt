evidence: the comment's point rests on something outside the comment that a reader could check without trusting the writer - a link, a quote from the article or docs, or a specific figure, date, law or spec tied to a public source
experience: the comment's point rests on a concrete first-hand account - something the writer (or their workplace or household) actually did, saw, or had happen to them, specific enough that you could picture it
opinion: the comment asserts a claim, judgment, prediction or joke and offers no support beyond the writer's own say-so or reasoning - nothing to check and no first-hand account
